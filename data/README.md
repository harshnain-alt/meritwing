# Option lists for the About you step

## States and union territories — `src/lib/states.ts`

All 28 states and 8 union territories of India, as named in the First Schedule to the
Constitution following the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 and the merger of Dadra
and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu on 26 January 2020. The list was written by hand and
cross-checked against secondary references on 2026-10-21; the Government's Know India site could
not be loaded to confirm it. Only these 36 values are accepted; the field has no free-text
option. A few common short or former names (e.g. UP, Orissa,
Pondicherry) are search aliases only.

## Business schools — `src/data/business-schools.json`

Generated by `scripts/build-datasets.mjs`. Students can always enter an institution that isn't
listed ("Use ‘…’"), so the list does not need to be complete.

There is no openly licensed, comprehensive register of Indian business schools, so this is an
**initial list**:

1. **NIRF India Rankings 2025: Management** — all 100 ranked institutions plus the 101–125 band,
   as published by the National Institutional Ranking Framework (Ministry of Education).
   <https://www.nirfindia.org/Rankings/2025/ManagementRanking.html> and
   `ManagementRanking150.html`, extracted 2026-10-21 into `data/sources/nirf-2025-management.tsv`.
   NIRF states no licence; only factual data (names and locations) is used, with attribution.
   NIRF lists only institutions that chose to participate, and names some by their parent
   university (e.g. “Indian Institute of Technology Bombay” for its management school).
2. **Manual additions** — `data/sources/business-schools-additions.tsv`, each checked on the
   institution's official website: Mesa School of Business, Indian School of Business
   (Hyderabad and Mohali), Faculty of Management Studies (University of Delhi), and Jamnalal Bajaj
   Institute of Management Studies.

Clean-up applied: typographic fixes only (backticks to apostrophes, spacing, dropping a “(PGDM)”
suffix); IIM Nagpur's unnamed entry is labelled with its city; the separately ranked PIBM PGDM
entry is merged into Pune Institute of Business Management. Search aliases (e.g. IIMA, SPJIMR,
NMIMS) are in `SCHOOL_ALIASES` in the script; “IIM/IIT/NIT + city” forms are added
automatically, and generated initialisms are kept only when they are unique (so “IIMA” finds
IIM Ahmedabad, not IIM Amritsar).

**Coverage:** 129 institutions. Many AICTE-approved MBA/PGDM institutions are not listed.

## Regenerating

```bash
node scripts/build-datasets.mjs
```

To add a business school, add a row to `data/sources/business-schools-additions.tsv` with the
official website you checked, then rerun the script.
